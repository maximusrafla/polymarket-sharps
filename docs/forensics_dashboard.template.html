Wallet forensics — the shape of the edge

Every conclusion in this project so far has been a mean in cents with a confidence interval. A mean of +2¢ can be five hundred quiet +2¢ bets, or one longshot that hit, or strongly positive in one price band and negative in another. This page computes the shape instead of the average, for every wallet deep enough to support the claim.

What the modelling found

Six results, in the order they were produced. Each was tested against the way it could most obviously be fake before being written down.

Does past edge predict future edge?

Each wallet's own history is split chronologically in half. The first half is the tip; the second half is the exam. Events appearing in both halves are dropped from both — the same resolution counted twice manufactures agreement out of nothing, which is exactly the trap the streak-following test fell into.
